pmf issue deferDECIDEDDEFERREDoption_is_defer, customer_acceptance_if_customer_visible
pmf issue closeRESOLVEDCLOSEDbrief_present_if_required, lesson_present
pmf issue retriageDEFERREDTRIAGEDreason_present
pmf issue reopenCLOSEDTRIAGEDreason_present

Aging clock starts on the detected date, whatever state the issue is in.

Escalated is computed: an issue in an open state for more than escalation.days is flagged.

A Sev 1 or Sev 2 must appear in the current RAID snapshot (pmf raid).

Changing severity is an event on the same state, not a transition; it is logged with a reason.

pmf risk assessIDENTIFIEDASSESSEDsingle_owner, valid_probability, valid_impact
pmf risk mitigateASSESSEDMITIGATINGmitigation_present

pmf risk occurASSESSEDOCCURREDlinked_issue_exists
pmf risk occurMITIGATINGOCCURREDlinked_issue_exists
pmf risk occurACCEPTEDOCCURREDlinked_issue_exists
pmf risk retireMITIGATINGRETIREDreason_present
pmf risk retireACCEPTEDRETIREDreason_present
pmf risk retireASSESSEDRETIREDreason_present

Risk score = probability x impact (1 to 25); the profile maps the score to a priority band and a review interval.

Re-scoring is an event on the same state. A risk not re-scored within its band's review interval is flagged stale.

A Critical or High risk cannot be ACCEPTED without a named approver and a note.

Change lifecycle

CommandFromToGuards (all must pass)
pmf change assessREQUESTEDASSESSEDsizing_present
pmf change approveASSESSEDAPPROVEDall_tier_approvers_signed, decision_ref_present
pmf change rejectASSESSEDREJECTEDreason_present
pmf change deferASSESSEDDEFERREDreason_present
pmf change implementAPPROVEDIMPLEMENTEDbudget_row_logged
pmf change verifyIMPLEMENTEDVERIFIEDverification_ref_present

No cost or schedule is committed before APPROVED. Work that starts earlier is a process failure, logged as an issue.

Tier = the highest of the cost tier and the schedule tier; the profile lists the approvers per tier.

When cumulative approved cost passes change_control.escalate_cumulative_pct of the baseline budget, pmf check warns that a formal re-baseline is due.

Gate lifecycle

CommandFromToGuards (all must pass)
pmf gate reviewPLANNEDREVIEWexit_criteria_listed
pmf gate goREVIEWGOall_criteria_met, no_blocking_open_issues
pmf gate conditionalREVIEWCONDITIONAL_GOwaiver_for_each_unmet_criterion, no_blocking_open_issues
pmf gate nogoREVIEWNO_GOissue_logged_for_each_unmet_criterion
pmf gate reviewNO_GOREVIEWreason_present

Planned gates and issue-triggered gates are listed apart; status reports never mix them.

A gate may be added after kickoff only as type issue-triggered, with the issue that caused it.

Gate on-time rate counts completed planned gates whose actual date is on or before the baseline date.

Sev 1 overlay

Extra obligations while a Sev 1 is open.

  • Exec notice sent the day it was detected
  • Daily war-room update logged until RESOLVED
  • Triage record with three options within the profile's Sev 1 cadence
  • Management brief before CLOSED
  • Retrospective one-pager after CLOSED

What each guard checks

GuardMeaning
acceptor_namedA named approver accepted the risk (and a note is given for Critical and High).
all_criteria_metcriteria_met covers every exit criterion.
all_tier_approvers_signedEvery approver the profile lists for the computed tier appears in approvers_signed.
brief_present_if_requiredIf the severity is in brief_required_for, briefs/B-NNN.md exists with all five sections filled.
budget_row_loggedThe cost delta is reflected in budget.csv (a forecast update dated on or after approval).
customer_acceptance_if_customer_visibleIf the record says Customer visible: yes, a customer acceptance reference is present.
decision_chosenThe record names the chosen option (A, B or C), who decided and when.
decision_record_has_recommendationThe record has a non-empty Recommendation line.
decision_record_has_three_optionsdecisions/DR-NNN.md exists and its Options table has rows A, B and C, each with schedule and cost filled.
decision_ref_presentdecision_ref points to an existing decision record.
decision_signed_if_waive_or_deferIf the choice is B or C, the record says Signed: yes.
exit_criteria_listedThe gate has at least one exit criterion in the profile.
has_required_fieldsid, title, description, phase, owner, severity and detected date are all filled.
issue_logged_for_each_unmet_criterionAn issue exists, with gate_found set to this gate, for each unmet criterion.
lesson_presentA one-line lesson is recorded.
linked_issue_existslinked_issue points to an existing issue in the issue register.
mitigation_presentA mitigation plan is written.
no_blocking_open_issuesNo open issue has a severity listed in the gate's requires_no_open_severity.
option_is_deferThe chosen option is C (defer).
option_is_fix_or_waiveThe chosen option is A (fix now) or B (waive with conditions).
reason_presentA reason is given and logged in the event note.
single_ownerExactly one named owner (no team names, no lists).
sizing_presentEffort, cost delta, schedule delta and risk note are filled.
valid_impactImpact is a whole number in the profile's scale.
valid_phasePhase is a phase id in the profile.
valid_probabilityProbability is a whole number in the profile's scale.
valid_severitySeverity is one of the levels in the profile.
verification_ref_presentA verification reference is recorded (evidence for a fix, the waiver conditions for a waiver).
waiver_for_each_unmet_criterionEvery unmet criterion is covered by a waiver_refs decision record marked Signed: yes.
